**How to Use This Dataset**

1. **Identify HTF bias** – look at Daily/4H.  
2. **Find OB/FVG** – check 1H/15m aligned with bias.  
3. **Confirm liquidity sweep or pool** – look for false break.  
4. **Check PD array** – ensure entry zone is correct.  
5. **Wait for kill/silver bullet** – align timing.  
6. **Enter** – with stop beyond the next OB/FVG or sweep.  
7. **Set TP** – 62‑79% retracement or next liquidity pool.  

This table should serve as a quick reference when analyzing charts and constructing high‑probability setups.
